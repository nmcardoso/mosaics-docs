DESI Legacy Imaging Surveys DR11 (ls11)¶

The DESI Legacy Imaging Surveys Data Release 11 (DR11) is the central wide-field photometric backbone of MOSAICS. It combines optical imaging from the Dark Energy Camera (DECam) on the Blanco 4-metre telescope in the South with the Beijing-Arizona Sky Survey (BASS; 90Prime on the Bok 2.3-metre) and the Mayall \(z\)-band Legacy Survey (MzLS; Mosaic-3 on the Mayall 4-metre) in the North, alongside forced infrared photometry from 6 years of unWISE coadds and reference astrometry and photometry from Gaia DR3.

In MOSAICS, ls11 is queried as the primary wide-area candidate store via mosaics-targets, which reads DuckDB-indexed sweep files hosted at NERSC (ls_dr11.sweep). Through its provides: block, a single extraction of ls11 delivers three surveys at once: optical Tractor photometry under the ls11_ prefix, unWISE forced infrared photometry (\(W1, W2, W3, W4\)) under the unwise_ prefix, and Gaia DR3 magnitudes (\(G, BP, RP\)) under the gaia3_ prefix. ls11 also supplies optical cutouts in \(g, r, z\) through the Legacy Survey Viewer (ls-dr11) and NERSC brick coadds.

Property

Value

What MOSAICS extracts

Catalogue: ls11 (photometric, nersc); Cutouts: lsv (g,r,z), nersc (g,r,z)

Archives

NERSC Legacy Survey portal (portal.nersc.gov), Legacy Survey Viewer (legacysurvey.org), NERSC coadd bricks (portal.nersc.gov), www.legacysurvey.org

Bands / wavelength range

g (CTIO/DECam.g), r (CTIO/DECam.r), i (CTIO/DECam.i), z (CTIO/DECam.z)

Used by

mosaics-targets (photometric extraction), mosaics-cutout full, mosaics-crossmatch, mosaics-query

Crossmatch radius

1 arcsec. Median delivered image quality about 1.3, 1.2 and 1.1 arcsec in g, r and z (Dey et al. 2019, arXiv:1804.08657); DR11 astrometric residuals “typically smaller than +-0.03 arcsec”, tied to Gaia DR3 (legacysurvey.org/dr11/description).

Configuration¶

The shipped file¶

Source: defs/surveys/ls11.yaml

# DESI Legacy Imaging Surveys DR11 -- the spine of the catalogue, and the
# source of three samples rather than one.
#
# The one survey extracted with SQL rather than crossmatched: uploading four
# hundred million rows to match them against the table they came from would be
# absurd. One extraction runs -- the cut photometric sample the production
# catalogue carries -- and the spectra it does not reach are matched back to the
# same table under `rescue_where` alone, so their counterparts are kept too.
#
# The tractor table publishes three surveys' measurements side by side: its own
# DECam/90Prime/Mosaic-3 optical photometry, unWISE forced photometry through
# the WISE passbands, and the Gaia DR3 photometry of its reference stars. They
# arrive in one query, so `provides:` below describes the last two under the
# prefixes they belong to instead of burying them in `ls11_`, and nothing has
# to ask Gaia or unWISE for them a second time.
#
# NOTE the `type`/`ref_cat` swap on Data Lab's copy of the tractor table --
# there the column named `ref_cat` holds the morphology in the south and the
# column named `type` the reference catalogue. defs/surveys/phot_ls_dr11_full.sql
# documents it at length. The sweep and tractor files NERSC serves name both
# correctly, and the query picks each by its value rather than its name, which is
# right for any of them.

survey_slug: ls11
name: DESI Legacy Imaging Surveys DR11

catalog:
  - name: ls11
    type: photometric
    adapter: nersc
    adapter_params:
      # The DR11 sweeps, from NERSC's portal, queried on this machine as
      # ls_dr11.sweep: the `nersc` provider downloads the 5-degree sweep files a
      # cell's sky needs -- once each, converted to Parquet sorted by
      # declination -- and runs the query over them in DuckDB. A sweep holds
      # only BRICK_PRIMARY rows and ~130 of the tractor catalogue's columns,
      # which is all this query reads, at a fraction of the per-brick tractor
      # files' bytes. With FROM ls_dr11.tractor the same text reads the tractor
      # files instead, on `nersc` or on Data Lab through `adl`. See
      # mosaics/query/sweep.py and mosaics/query/providers/nersc.py.
      table: ls_dr11.sweep
      dialect: limit          # DuckDB, as Data Lab's PostgreSQL before it
      use_async: false        # there is no job to submit; DuckDB answers here

# Crossmatch: the radius a table of positions is matched within, and what it rests on.
crossmatch:
  radius: 1 arcsec
  radius_note: >-
    Median delivered image quality about 1.3, 1.2 and 1.1 arcsec in g, r and z (Dey et al.
    2019, arXiv:1804.08657); DR11 astrometric residuals "typically smaller than +-0.03
    arcsec", tied to Gaia DR3 (legacysurvey.org/dr11/description).

catalog_filters:
  # in file: z 23.0, r 23.9, g 24.7 AB -> nMgy. Legacy Survey writes flux 0
  # with ivar 0, never null, so "missing" means ivar = 0.
  ls11: >-
    ls11_flux_z >= 0.631
    OR (COALESCE(ls11_flux_z_ivar,0) = 0 AND ls11_flux_r >= 0.275)
    OR (COALESCE(ls11_flux_z_ivar,0) = 0 AND COALESCE(ls11_flux_r_ivar,0) = 0 AND ls11_flux_g >= 0.132)
  ls11_note: >-
    5 sigma point source, AB. DR10's per-band depths are g 24.7, r 23.9,
    z 23.0 (Legacy Surveys DR10 description); z is the shallowest of the
    three the stack fills, so it is the one that decides whether an object
    is in the imaging at all. DR11 is the same instrument and cadence.
    `linear` photometry here means nanomaggies on the 22.5 AB zero point,
    so the number needs no conversion to be compared against it.
  # note moved from unwise.yaml: forced photometry has a W1 flux for every
  # row, so no fallback band is needed.
  unwise: unwise_flux_w1 >= 8.3
  unwise_note: >-
    5 sigma, VEGA, five-year coadds (Meisner et al. 2019). Vega is now the
    awkward scale here rather than the convenient one: it was chosen to
    compare against `wise_mag_w1`, but the photometry this survey carries is
    `unwise_flux_w1`, which is AB nanomaggies. The AB equivalent is
    17.5 + 2.699 = 20.199 mag, which on the 22.5 nanomaggy zero point is
    10**((22.5 - 20.199) / 2.5) = 8.3 nanomaggies -- using the same WISEAB1
    offset defs/surveys/wise.yaml records.

cutout:
  # No single-band keys: CDS registers Legacy Survey HiPS for DR10 only
  # (MOCServer, 2026-09-05), and DR10 is a different data release, which is
  # what `ls10` is for. Adding `CDS/P/DESI-Legacy-Surveys/DR10/g` here would
  # put DR10 pixels in a `cutout_ls11_g` column.
  - adapter: lsv          # VERIFIED LIVE 2026-08-25
    adapter_params:
      layer: ls-dr11
      bands: grz
    bands: [g, r, z]
  - adapter: nersc        # bulk coadds, for dense fields
    adapter_params:
      bands: grz
      data_release: dr11
    bands: [g, r, z]
    cost:
      latency_s: 0.5
      bandwidth_bps: 25000000
      concurrency: 8
      render_seconds: 0.05

photometric_system:
  filters:
    # Optical only. W1-W4 are unWISE forced photometry, not a Legacy Survey
    # measurement, and now live under the `unwise` prefix in `provides:`.
    #
    # DECam is the default because it covers the larger footprint, not
    # because it covers all of it: Legacy Survey north is 90Prime (g, r) and
    # Mosaic-3 (z). See `filter_variants` below for the rows that are.
    g: CTIO/DECam.g
    r: CTIO/DECam.r
    i: CTIO/DECam.i
    z: CTIO/DECam.z
  filter_variants:
    # DR11 publishes no column saying which camera saw an object, but it does
    # publish the rule by which one was chosen, and `ls11_hemisphere` is that
    # rule evaluated per row: BASS and MzLS count only north of Dec 32.375
    # and north of the Galactic plane, DECam otherwise. See
    # `_hemisphere_expressions` in mosaics/pipelines/targets/production.py.
    #
    # SVO does carry both northern instruments, checked live 2026-09-09
    # against the same PhotCalID/AB route the filters phase uses. Their pivot
    # wavelengths differ from DECam's by 0.8% (g), 0.4% (r) and 0.3% (z),
    # which is the ~1-2% these columns used to be wrong by in the north --
    # flam goes as 1/pivot^2. fnu is unaffected either way: a nanomaggy is
    # m_AB = 22.5 whichever camera measured it.
    column: ls11_hemisphere
    cases:
      N:
        g: BOK/BASS.g            # 90Prime on the Bok 2.3m
        r: BOK/BASS.r
        z: KPNO/MzLS.z           # Mosaic-3 on the Mayall 4m
        # No i: DR11's i band is south-only, so a northern row has no i
        # flux for the DECam curve above to be the wrong one for.
        # No w1-w4 to worry about either: they belong to `unwise` now, and
        # unWISE is one spacecraft over the whole sky.
  measurements:
    # Nanomaggies, AB by construction, inverse variances. The only survey
    # whose fluxes are unclipped at the faint end, which is why nothing in
    # the homogenisation ever guards a linear conversion on positivity.
    - system: linear
      apertures: [default]
      scale: 3.631            # uJy per nanomaggy, exact: a nanomaggy is m_AB = 22.5
      error_kind: ivar
  pixel_unit: nanomaggy
  pixel_unit_note: Legacy Survey coadds are nanomaggies by construction.

target_selection:
  catalog_name: ls11
  query: phot_ls_dr11_full.sql
  ra_column: ra
  dec_column: dec
  name: ls11.parquet
  # A spectrum with no row of this extraction within the match radius is
  # matched again against ls_dr11.tractor under these conditions alone
  # (step logic/ls11_rescue), so a spectroscopic object whose photometry
  # fails the cuts above keeps its counterpart instead of being dropped.
  # From NERSC that reads single rows, by range request, out of the sweeps
  # the extraction already indexed. Deduplication only: one primary
  # detection per object -- which every sweep row is -- never a DUP, and
  # NULL-safe for the type/ref_cat Data Lab's copy transposes.
  rescue_where: >-
    brick_primary = 1
    AND (ref_cat IS NULL OR ref_cat != 'DUP')
    AND (type IS NULL OR type != 'DUP')

# Two other surveys' photometry, carried by the same query. Each is a full
# photometry description minus a `source:` -- the extraction above is the
# source -- and each replaces any standalone description of the same prefix,
# which is what removes a crossmatch rather than adding one.
provides:
  unwise:
    # unWISE NEO-R forced photometry: the WISE passbands measured at the
    # optical position, in AB nanomaggies on the same 22.5 zero point as the
    # optical fluxes beside them.
    #
    # Deliberately NOT the `wise` prefix. AllWISE is a different data product
    # -- profile-fit Vega magnitudes from a different reduction -- and
    # defs/surveys/wise.yaml keeps it. Applying that file's Vega-to-AB
    # offsets to these already-AB fluxes is the most common error in
    # multi-survey work, and giving the two different prefixes is what makes
    # it impossible here.
    filters:
      w1: WISE/WISE.W1
      w2: WISE/WISE.W2
      w3: WISE/WISE.W3
      w4: WISE/WISE.W4
    measurements:
      - system: linear
        apertures: [default]
        scale: 3.631          # uJy per nanomaggy, exact, as for the optical
        error_kind: ivar
    # unWISE measures at the optical position and publishes no source
    # identifier, so there is no `unwise_id` and no separation to record.
    #
    # This flag is true for essentially every row, and that is the honest
    # answer rather than a broken one: unWISE covers the whole sky and the
    # Legacy Survey forces photometry at every optical position, so every
    # target really does have a W1 measurement. Checked live -- 3,000 of
    # 3,000 rows in a test box, nobs_w1 between 274 and 296, no zeros. The
    # fluxes go negative at the faint end, as forced photometry should, so
    # "has a measurement" and "is detected" are different questions and only
    # the first is what a sample flag asks.
    detection_column: unwise_flux_w1

  gaia3:
    # Gaia DR3 photometry of the tractor catalogue's reference stars. A
    # subset of what defs/surveys/gaia3.yaml describes -- no radial
    # velocities, no DSC class probabilities -- but it arrives free with a
    # query that was going to run anyway, so the standalone crossmatch
    # against gaia_dr3.gaia_source is replaced rather than run beside it.
    #
    # The measurement differs from gaia3.yaml's and has to: that file reads
    # `gaia3_flux_g` in e-/s, and DR11 republishes magnitudes, not fluxes.
    # Same passband, different calibration, because a different table
    # published it.
    filters:
      g: GAIA/GAIA3.G
      bp: GAIA/GAIA3.Gbp
      rp: GAIA/GAIA3.Grp
    vega_offsets:
      # m_AB - m_Vega, measured rather than quoted: derived live 2026-09-11
      # from 144 gaia_dr3.gaia_source rows carrying both the VEGAMAG
      # magnitude and the e-/s flux, as ZP_AB - ZP_Vega.
      #
      #   ZP_Vega, from median(mag + 2.5*log10(flux)), spread 0.0000:
      #     G 25.6874   BP 25.3385   RP 24.7479
      #   ZP_AB, from gaia3.yaml's own c_nu constants, 2.5*log10(3631e6/c_nu):
      #     G 25.8012   BP 25.3540   RP 25.1042
      #
      # The ZP_AB row reproduces the 25.8010 / 25.3540 / 25.1040 that
      # gaia3.yaml quotes, so the two files agree on the AB scale and differ
      # only in which quantity they start from.
      g: 0.1138
      bp: 0.0155
      rp: 0.3563
    measurements:
      - system: vega_mag
        apertures: [default]
    # There is no `gaia3_id` to fall back on -- see the extraction query for
    # why one would be lossy -- so the detection indicator is named here. The
    # G magnitude is NULL exactly when the row has no Gaia counterpart, which
    # is what the flag means. Measured live: 20.8% of a test box.
    detection_column: gaia3_mag_g

# The projection for the *relational* crossmatch against ls_dr11.sweep,
# joined on ls_id rather than on position: the candidate table already
# carries the ls_id it came from.
#
# Kept in step with defs/surveys/phot_ls_dr11_full.sql, alias for alias -- the
# two describe the same columns of the same table by two routes, and the
# extraction is the one production actually reads (it is joined first, so the
# first-claim rule drops this crossmatch's copies). The four columns the
# extraction derives with CASE are absent below and noted where they belong.
#
# `'ls11' AS sample` is not a remote column -- `sample` is stamped by pandas
# after the crossmatch runs, not selected here.
column_mapping:
  # Legacy Survey's own optical photometry, geometry and quality flags.
  ls_id_dr11: 'id'                              # unique LS object ID
  brickid: 'brick_id'                           # brick ID, [1, 662174]
  # objid: 'objid'                                # catalog object number within the brick
  ra: 'ra'                                      # right ascension at equinox J2000, deg
  dec: 'dec'                                    # declination at equinox J2000, deg
  ebv: 'ebv'                                    # Galactic extinction E(B-V) from SFD98, mag
  flux_g: 'flux_g'                              # model flux in g, nanomaggy
  flux_r: 'flux_r'                              # model flux in r, nanomaggy
  flux_i: 'flux_i'                              # model flux in i, nanomaggy
  flux_z: 'flux_z'                              # model flux in z, nanomaggy
  flux_ivar_g: 'flux_g_ivar'                    # inverse variance of flux_g, 1/nanomaggy^2
  flux_ivar_r: 'flux_r_ivar'                    # inverse variance of flux_r, 1/nanomaggy^2
  flux_ivar_i: 'flux_i_ivar'                    # inverse variance of flux_i, 1/nanomaggy^2
  flux_ivar_z: 'flux_z_ivar'                    # inverse variance of flux_z, 1/nanomaggy^2
  mw_transmission_g: 'mw_transmission_g'        # Galactic transmission in g, linear [0, 1]
  mw_transmission_r: 'mw_transmission_r'        # Galactic transmission in r, linear [0, 1]
  mw_transmission_i: 'mw_transmission_i'        # Galactic transmission in i, linear [0, 1]
  mw_transmission_z: 'mw_transmission_z'        # Galactic transmission in z, linear [0, 1]

  mw_transmission_w2: 'mw_transmission_w2'      # Galactic transmission in W2, linear [0, 1]
  mw_transmission_w3: 'mw_transmission_w3'      # Galactic transmission in W3, linear [0, 1]
  mw_transmission_w4: 'mw_transmission_w4'      # Galactic transmission in W4, linear [0, 1]
  shape_r: 'shape_r'                            # half-light radius of galaxy model, arcsec
  shape_e1: 'shape_e1'                          # ellipticity component 1 of galaxy model
  shape_e2: 'shape_e2'                          # ellipticity component 2 of galaxy model

  nobs_r: 'nobs_r'                              # number of contributing images in r (not profile-weighted)
  nobs_i: 'nobs_i'                              # number of contributing images in i (not profile-weighted)

  fracflux_i: 'fracflux_i'                      # profile-weighted fraction of flux from other sources in i

  fracmasked_g: 'fracmasked_g'                  # profile-weighted fraction of masked pixels in g
  fracmasked_r: 'fracmasked_r'                  # profile-weighted fraction of masked pixels in r

  fracmasked_z: 'fracmasked_z'                  # profile-weighted fraction of masked pixels in z

  fracin_i: 'fracin_i'                          # fraction of source flux within the blob in i
  fracin_z: 'fracin_z'                          # fraction of source flux within the blob in z
  rchisq_g: 'rchisq_g'                          # profile-weighted reduced chi^2 of the model fit in g

  psfsize_r: 'psfsize_r'                        # weighted average PSF FWHM in r, arcsec

  maskbits: 'maskbits'                          # bitwise mask against the DR11 coadd maskbits maps

    rchisq_w2: 'rchisq_w2'              # profile-weighted reduced chi^2 of the W2 fit
    rchisq_w3: 'rchisq_w3'              # profile-weighted reduced chi^2 of the W3 fit
    rchisq_w4: 'rchisq_w4'              # profile-weighted reduced chi^2 of the W4 fit
    psfdepth_w1: 'psfdepth_w1'          # point-source 5-sigma depth in W1, 1/nanomaggy^2
    psfdepth_w2: 'psfdepth_w2'          # point-source 5-sigma depth in W2, 1/nanomaggy^2
    wisemask_w1: 'wisemask_w1'          # W1 bitmask (see DR11 bitmasks page)
    wisemask_w2: 'wisemask_w2'          # W2 bitmask (see DR11 bitmasks page)

  # Three families of column are deliberately absent here rather than
  # forgotten, and all for one reason: a crossmatch projects *columns*, and
  # each of these has to be computed per row.
  #
  # `ls11_type` and `ls11_ref_cat` -- either source column may hold either
  # value depending on the hemisphere, so both are picked on the value. See
  # the transposition note in defs/surveys/phot_ls_dr11_full.sql.
  #
  # Every gaia3_* column. Every
  # Gaia column has to be gated on an actual Gaia match -- the tractor files,
  # and Data Lab after them, write 0, not NULL, for a row that has none -- so
  # each one is a CASE expression in
  # defs/surveys/phot_ls_dr11_full.sql. A crossmatch projects *columns*, and
  # cannot express the gate, so mapping them here would reintroduce exactly
  # the 0-magnitude sources the gate exists to prevent. The extraction is the
  # route that carries Gaia, and it is the one production joins first anyway.

Target-selection query (defs/surveys/phot_ls_dr11_full.sql):

-- MASKBITS veto bits 0,1,10,12,13,17,18,19 -> 930819
-- SATUR+ALLMASK bits 2,3,4,14 and 5,6,7,15 -> 49404
-- FITBITS veto bits 4 (FROZEN),10 (WALKER),11 (RUNNER) -> 3088

-- Wide photometric extraction for the targets pipeline.
-- Fills the phot/ls11 store cell by cell through fetch_cells, against provider
-- nersc: DuckDB over the DR11 *sweep* files of each cell's sky, served as
-- ls_dr11.sweep (mosaics/query/sweep.py). A sweep holds only BRICK_PRIMARY rows
-- and ~130 of the tractor catalogue's columns, which is everything selected
-- below, at a fraction of the per-brick tractor files' bytes.
-- Carries fluxes not magnitudes; ls11_mag_r is computed downstream as
-- 22.5 - 2.5*log10(flux_r / mw_transmission_r).
--
-- Not carried, because only the tractor files and the -extra sweeps publish
-- them: ngood_*, mjd_min/max, psfdepth_w3/w4, and Gaia's phot_*_n_obs,
-- phot_variable_flag, astrometric_excess_noise_sig, astrometric_n_obs_al,
-- astrometric_n_good_obs_al, astrometric_weight_al, a_g_val and e_bp_min_rp_val.
-- The -extra sweeps are twice the size of the standard ones, and nothing
-- downstream read those columns.
--
-- Every column below also exists in the tractor table under the same name, so
-- with FROM ls_dr11.tractor this text reads the per-brick tractor files -- on
-- nersc, or on Data Lab through provider adl (its Query Manager, not TAP: TAP
-- rejects GREATEST, as mosaics/report/db_constraints/run.py documents).

-- !! On Data Lab's ls_dr11.tractor the contents of `type` and `ref_cat` are
-- transposed in the southern half of the table, and only the southern half.
-- Verified live 2026-09-11 against four boxes:
--
--   dec +10, dec -20  (south, DECam)       ref_cat = PSF/REX/...   type = G3
--   dec +45, dec +60  (north, BASS/MzLS)   ref_cat = G3            type = PSF/REX/...
--
-- The sweep and tractor files NERSC serves name both correctly. Every use below
-- still picks per row, on the value, so the text is right against any of them:
-- the morphological codes ('PSF', 'REX', 'DEV', 'EXP', 'SER', 'DUP') and the
-- reference-catalogue codes ('G2'/'G3'/'GE' for Gaia, 'T2' for Tycho-2,
-- 'L3'/'L4' for the SGA) are disjoint sets, so "whichever of the two holds a
-- morphology code is the morphology" is exact and needs no declination cut.
--
-- Every predicate below is also NULL-safe. A blank reference catalogue is NULL
-- here, as on Data Lab, and `ref_cat != 'DUP'` on a NULL is NULL: the
-- `type != 'DUP'` this file used to carry discarded every row without a Gaia or
-- SGA match -- 930 of 22,991 rows survived a test box where 22,985 should have.

  psfdepth_w1                 AS unwise_psfdepth_w1,
  psfdepth_w2                 AS unwise_psfdepth_w2,

  wisemask_w1                 AS unwise_wisemask_w1,
  wisemask_w2                 AS unwise_wisemask_w2,

  -- identifier -- hence `coadd_id`, so nothing mistakes it for `unwise_id`

  wise_coadd_id               AS unwise_coadd_id,

  -- Gaia DR3, as the Legacy Survey catalogue republishes it for its reference
  -- stars. Aliases match defs/surveys/gaia3.yaml so a column means the same
  -- thing whichever route filled it.
  --
  -- !! Every column here is gated on an actual Gaia match, and that is a
  -- correctness fix rather than caution. The catalogue files write **0, not
  -- NULL**, for a row with no Gaia counterpart, and Data Lab copies them --
  -- verified live, 2,879 of 3,000 rows in a Data Lab test box carried

  -- magnitude of zero is not a missing value; it is the brightest object in the
  -- sky. Ungated, ~96% of the catalogue would arrive as 0-magnitude Gaia sources
  -- and be homogenised into enormous fluxes.
  --
  -- The gate reads BOTH columns because either may hold the reference
  -- catalogue; see the transposition note at the top. Testing only one silently
  -- empties the Gaia sample over half of Data Lab's sky -- measured, a
  -- whole-sky dry run returned 0 Gaia matches in 1,060 rows when this tested
  -- `type` alone.
  --
  -- Spelled out per column rather than hoisted into a CTE: the WHERE clause
  -- has to stay at the top level for mosaics.report.db_constraints.decompose to
  -- find it.
  --
  -- There is deliberately no `gaia3_id`. The exact Gaia source_id is
  -- `ls11_ref_id` wherever `ls11_ref_cat` is a Gaia code: it is int64 there and
  -- stays exact, whereas a nullable copy comes back from the fetch as float64,
  -- which carries 53 bits against a source_id of ~2e18. Measured: 527 of 1,060
  -- values wrong by +-128. One lossless identifier beats two, one of which lies.
  CASE WHEN gaia_phot_g_mean_flux_over_error > 0
    THEN 2.5 / LN(10.0) / gaia_phot_g_mean_flux_over_error
  END                         AS gaia3_mag_g_err,
  CASE WHEN gaia_phot_bp_mean_flux_over_error > 0
    THEN 2.5 / LN(10.0) / gaia_phot_bp_mean_flux_over_error
  END                         AS gaia3_mag_bp_err,
  CASE WHEN gaia_phot_rp_mean_flux_over_error > 0
    THEN 2.5 / LN(10.0) / gaia_phot_rp_mean_flux_over_error
  END                         AS gaia3_mag_rp_err,

  END                         AS gaia3_mag_g,
  CASE WHEN ref_cat IN ('G2', 'G3', 'GE') OR type IN ('G2', 'G3', 'GE')
    THEN gaia_phot_bp_mean_mag
  END                         AS gaia3_mag_bp,
  CASE WHEN ref_cat IN ('G2', 'G3', 'GE') OR type IN ('G2', 'G3', 'GE')
    THEN gaia_phot_rp_mean_mag
  END                         AS gaia3_mag_rp,

  CASE WHEN ref_cat IN ('G2', 'G3', 'GE') OR type IN ('G2', 'G3', 'GE')
    THEN gaia_phot_bp_mean_flux_over_error
  END                         AS gaia3_flux_bp_snr,
  CASE WHEN ref_cat IN ('G2', 'G3', 'GE') OR type IN ('G2', 'G3', 'GE')
    THEN gaia_phot_rp_mean_flux_over_error
  END                         AS gaia3_flux_rp_snr,
  CASE WHEN ref_cat IN ('G2', 'G3', 'GE') OR type IN ('G2', 'G3', 'GE')
    THEN gaia_phot_bp_rp_excess_factor
  END                         AS gaia3_bp_rp_excess,
  CASE WHEN ref_cat IN ('G2', 'G3', 'GE') OR type IN ('G2', 'G3', 'GE')
    THEN gaia_duplicated_source
  END                         AS gaia3_duplicated,
  CASE WHEN ref_cat IN ('G2', 'G3', 'GE') OR type IN ('G2', 'G3', 'GE')
    THEN gaia_astrometric_excess_noise
  END                         AS gaia3_excess_noise,
  CASE WHEN ref_cat IN ('G2', 'G3', 'GE') OR type IN ('G2', 'G3', 'GE')
    THEN gaia_astrometric_sigma5d_max
  END                         AS gaia3_astrom_sigma5d_max,
  CASE WHEN ref_cat IN ('G2', 'G3', 'GE') OR type IN ('G2', 'G3', 'GE')
    THEN gaia_astrometric_params_solved
  END                         AS gaia3_astrom_params
FROM
  ls_dr11.sweep
WHERE
  -- True for every sweep row; kept so the text selects the same rows from
  -- ls_dr11.tractor, which holds the non-primary copies too.
  brick_primary = 1
  -- Either column may hold the morphology, so DUP is excluded from both; a
  -- NULL keeps the row rather than silently dropping it. Sweeps do carry DUP
  -- rows: a brick-primary reference source with no Tractor fit of its own.
  AND (ref_cat IS NULL OR ref_cat != 'DUP')
  AND (type IS NULL OR type != 'DUP')
  -- S2b: foreground / veto masks (bright stars, SGA, clusters, LMC/SMC, bailout)
  AND (maskbits & 930819) = 0
  -- S2b: saturation and ALLMASK, required clear in at least three of four bands
  AND (maskbits & 49404) = 0
  -- S2c: fit-quality bits
  AND (fitbits & 3088) = 0
  -- S2a: observed and with a defined error in >= 3 optical bands
  AND (
    (CASE WHEN nobs_g > 0 AND flux_ivar_g > 0 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)
    + (CASE WHEN nobs_r > 0 AND flux_ivar_r > 0 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)
    + (CASE WHEN nobs_i > 0 AND flux_ivar_i > 0 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)
    + (CASE WHEN nobs_z > 0 AND flux_ivar_z > 0 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)
  ) >= 3
  -- S2c: FRACMASKED < 0.4, FRACIN > 0.3, FRACFLUX < 5 in >= 2 bands
  AND (
    (CASE WHEN fracmasked_g < 0.4 AND fracin_g > 0.3 AND fracflux_g < 5.0 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)
    + (CASE WHEN fracmasked_r < 0.4 AND fracin_r > 0.3 AND fracflux_r < 5.0 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)
    + (CASE WHEN fracmasked_i < 0.4 AND fracin_i > 0.3 AND fracflux_i < 5.0 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)
    + (CASE WHEN fracmasked_z < 0.4 AND fracin_z > 0.3 AND fracflux_z < 5.0 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)
  ) >= 2
  AND dchisq_1 > 25.0
  -- PSF-model detection significance
  AND rchisq_r < 20.0
  AND (shape_r < 100.0 OR ref_cat = 'PSF' OR type = 'PSF')
  -- S2d: S/N >= 5 in >= 2 optical bands
  AND (
    (CASE WHEN flux_g * SQRT(flux_ivar_g) >= 5 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)
    + (CASE WHEN flux_r * SQRT(flux_ivar_r) >= 5 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)
    + (CASE WHEN flux_i * SQRT(flux_ivar_i) >= 5 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)
    + (CASE WHEN flux_z * SQRT(flux_ivar_z) >= 5 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)
  ) >= 2
  -- S6: foreground extinction
  AND ebv < 0.3
  -- S2d: depth-matched magnitude limit (global term; local term applied in code)
  AND (
    22.5 - 2.5*LOG10(GREATEST(flux_z/mw_transmission_z, 1e-6)) < 21.0
    OR 22.5 - 2.5*LOG10(GREATEST(flux_r/mw_transmission_r, 1e-6)) < 21.5
  )

Common changes¶

  • Relax or remove post-query flux thresholds in your entrypoint YAML:

    overrides:
  surveys/ls11:
    catalog_filters:
      ls11: null
      unwise: null

    The shipped filters enforce \(5\sigma\) point-source optical detection (\(z \ge 0.631\,\text{nMgy}\), falling back to \(r \ge 0.275\,\text{nMgy}\) and \(g \ge 0.132\,\text{nMgy}\) when inverse variance is zero) and an unWISE detection threshold (\(W1 \ge 8.3\,\text{nMgy}\), equivalent to \(W1 \le 20.199\) AB mag). Setting them to null admits faint candidates down to the raw sweep catalog limits.

  • Switch cutout providers between the Legacy Survey Viewer (lsv) and bulk NERSC coadd downloads (nersc) depending on query density and network bandwidth.

  • Disable the bundled unwise or gaia3 photometric outputs in provides: if you wish to run standalone crossmatches against AllWISE or full Gaia DR3 catalogues instead of using the Tractor-republished quantities.

Data served by the archive¶

  • North/South resolve boundary and camera passbands: DR11 does not carry an explicit camera identification column, but follows a strict territorial resolve rule: BASS (\(g, r\)) and MzLS (\(z\)) govern the sky north of \(\text{Dec} = 32.375^\circ\) and Galactic latitude \(b > 0^\circ\); DECam governs all remaining sky. MOSAICS tags this split per row in ls11_hemisphere ('N' vs 'S') and assigns camera-specific transmission curves (BOK/BASS, KPNO/MzLS vs CTIO/DECam). Northern pivot wavelengths differ from DECam by 0.8% (\(g\)), 0.4% (\(r\)), and 0.3% (\(z\)). The \(i\) band exists only in the southern DECam footprint.

  • Three surveys in a single query (provides:): The sweep files co-publish optical Tractor fits, unWISE forced fluxes, and Gaia DR3 reference magnitudes. MOSAICS partitions these columns into distinct prefixes (ls11_, unwise_, and gaia3_), eliminating the need to upload hundreds of millions of candidates for separate Gaia or unWISE crossmatches. unWISE measurements are in linear AB nanomaggies on the 22.5 zero point (not Vega magnitudes), preventing common multi-survey calibration errors.

  • Transposition of type and ref_cat on Data Lab: On the Astro Data Lab ls_dr11.tractor table, the column contents of type (morphology: PSF, REX, DEV, EXP, SER, DUP) and ref_cat (reference catalog: G2, G3, GE, T2, L3, L4) are transposed in the southern sky (\(\text{Dec} < 32.375^\circ\)). To ensure identical results across NERSC sweeps and Data Lab, queries determine morphology and reference catalog based on disjoint value sets rather than column names.

  • NULL safety on reference catalogue: Unmatched reference catalog entries are NULL. Testing ref_cat != 'DUP' in SQL evaluates to NULL (falsy) on null values, silently discarding ~96% of rows lacking a Gaia or SGA counterpart. Queries explicitly guard predicates with (ref_cat IS NULL OR ref_cat != 'DUP') AND (type IS NULL OR type != 'DUP').

  • Gating zero-magnitude Gaia records: Tractor catalogues record 0, not NULL, for sources without a Gaia counterpart. A magnitude of 0 represents the brightest star in the sky; without explicit gating, non-Gaia objects would be homogenised into catastrophic multi-jansky fluxes. Queries explicitly gate all gaia3_* columns using CASE WHEN ref_cat IN ('G2','G3','GE') OR type IN ('G2','G3','GE') ....

  • Exact Gaia source IDs: The exact Gaia DR3 identifier is preserved in ls11_ref_id as an int64. A separate nullable gaia3_id column is avoided because converting 64-bit integer identifiers to 64-bit floating point in intermediate tables corrupts least-significant bits.

  • Linear nanomaggies and inverse variance: Fluxes are linear nanomaggies (\(1\,\text{nanomaggy} = 3.631\,\mu\text{Jy}\), exact AB zero point 22.5) with inverse variances (ivar). Fluxes are unclipped and can be negative at the faint end, as expected for unbiased forced photometry.

The Legacy Surveys consist of three individual and complementary projects: the Dark Energy Camera Legacy Survey (DECaLS), the Beijing-Arizona Sky Survey (BASS), and the Mayall z-band Legacy Survey (MzLS). DECaLS, BASS and MzLS together include data obtained, respectively, at the Blanco telescope, Cerro Tololo Inter-American Observatory, NSF’s NOIRLab; the Bok telescope, Steward Observatory, University of Arizona; and the Mayall telescope, Kitt Peak National Observatory, NSF’s NOIRLab. The Legacy Surveys project is honored to be permitted to conduct astronomical research on Iolkam Du’ag (Kitt Peak), a mountain with particular significance to the Tohono O’odham Nation. The DESI Legacy Imaging Surveys are funded by the U.S. Department of Energy Office of Science, the National Science Foundation, and participating institutions.
