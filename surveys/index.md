# Survey Reference

`mosaics` ships definitions for **32 surveys** under `defs/surveys/<slug>.yaml`, including the two Rubin/LSST Data Previews (`lsstdp1`, `lsstdp2`), which only `mosaics-crossmatch` reads.

Each survey page documents:

1. **Identity & Capabilities:** what pipelines (`targets`, `crossmatch`, `cutout`, `spectrum`) the survey participates in, its archive endpoint, and its output column prefix.
2. **Narrative & Provenance:** the astronomical data release, selection rationale, unit conventions, and caveats.
3. **Photometric / Spectroscopic System:** filter bands, effective wavelengths, zero-points, magnitude system, or wavelength range and resolving power.
4. **Target Selection & Catalogue Configuration:** how `mosaics` queries the survey in stage 1 (`target_selection`) and stages 3–4 (`catalog`), including quality constraints and post-fetch `catalog_filters`.
5. **Cutout & Spectra Configuration:** image dimensions, pixel scales, HiPS/LSV/NERSC parameters, and 1D spectrum adapter rules.
6. **Shipped Configuration & SQL:** the verbatim `defs/surveys/<slug>.yaml` and any referenced `defs/surveys/*.sql` query.
7. **Source Data Models:** the full schema (column name, type, unit, UCD, and archive description) of every upstream archive table `mosaics` queries for the survey (plus the primary science source catalogue for cutout-only surveys), highlighting the columns selected by `mosaics` and their mapped output column names.
