Targets Production Catalogue Schema¶

mosaics-targets writes the final crossmatched, homogenised catalogue to <output>/targets.parquet (or .lance), stamped with schema version metadata key mosaics_catalog_schema.

1. Core anchor and summary columns¶

Every row represents one unique astronomical source anchored on the resolved Legacy Survey DR11 primary catalogue (ls11) and/or spectroscopic selections:

Column

Type

Units

Description

id

int64

—

Canonical target identifier (ls11_id when matched to DR11, or deterministic hash for unrescued spectroscopic-only sources).

ra

float64

deg

Best available right ascension (ICRS).

dec

float64

deg

Best available declination (ICRS).

z

float32

—

Best spectroscopic redshift, selected across all matching spectroscopic surveys in order of spectroscopic_system.redshift_rank.

z_err

float32

—

\(1\sigma\) uncertainty on z.

z_survey

string

—

Survey slug that contributed z (desi1, sdss17, lamost11, 6dfgs, 2dfgrs, euclidq1s, hasp).

fov

float32

arcsec

Recommended square cutout edge length in arcseconds, from the Legacy Survey shape and r magnitude of the target (ellipse form, circle form where the ellipse is missing, or the half-light ellipse alone with --hle). A row with a missing shape or magnitude measurement is not in the catalogue; a row whose size is zero is, with an empty (null) fov, and mosaics-cutout gives it a default field of view (--default-fov, or the median of the valid values). See Field of view; mosaics-cutout needs --fov-unit arcsec to read it.

split

string

—

Spatial HEALPix split assignment (train, val, test) preventing neighbour leakage across splits.

2. Per-survey detection flags¶

For every photometric and spectroscopic survey prefix <prefix> (ls11, gaia3, unwise, denis3, euclidq1p, euclidq1s, galex, hsc3, hst2, kids3, sdss16, skymapper4, twomass, ukidss9, ukidss11, vhs5, wise, 2dfgrs, 6dfgs, desi1, hasp, lamost11, sdss17), a boolean column <prefix> (bool, non-nullable) indicates whether the target has valid measurements from that survey after applying post-hoc catalog_filters.

3. Homogenised photometric columns¶

Every photometric measurement block declared under photometric_system.measurements (and provides.<prefix>.measurements) is homogenised onto a uniform physical scale:

  • AB magnitudes: <prefix>_mag_<aperture>_<band> and <prefix>_mag_err_<aperture>_<band> (or <prefix>_mag_<band> when aperture is default), in AB magnitudes (float32). Native Vega magnitudes (vega_mag) are shifted by +vega_offsets[band], and Lupton asinh_mag columns are converted to linear flux and AB magnitudes.

  • Linear fluxes: <prefix>_flux_<aperture>_<band> and <prefix>_flux_err_<aperture>_<band>, converted to microjanskys (\(\mu\text{Jy}\)) (float32), where \(1\ \text{nanomaggy} = 3.6307805\ \mu\text{Jy}\) and \(m_{\text{AB}} = 23.90 - 2.5 \log_{10}(f_{\mu\text{Jy}})\). Native inverse variances (error_kind: ivar) are converted to \(1\sigma\) flux uncertainties in \(\mu\text{Jy}\).

4. Spectroscopic survey columns¶

Each spectroscopic survey prefix <spec_prefix> (desi1, sdss17, lamost11, 6dfgs, 2dfgrs, hasp, euclidq1s) contributes its own prefixed columns:

  • <spec_prefix>_id, <spec_prefix>_ra, <spec_prefix>_dec

  • <spec_prefix>_z, <spec_prefix>_z_err

  • Survey-specific fibre/instrument metadata (sdss17_plate, sdss17_mjd, sdss17_fiberid, lamost11_obsid, sixdfgs_access_url, hst_access_url, euclid_access_url) required downstream by mosaics-spectrum.
